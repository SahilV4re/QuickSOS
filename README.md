



## Project Overview
Our SOS application empowers users to summon help instantly in critical situations. Key features include managing emergency contacts, personalizing SOS messages, accessing essential helpline numbers, and reviewing first-aid guidance. The centerpiece is Emergency Mode: once triggered, the app automatically sends your real-time location and a distress alert to all registered contacts.

## Operating Instructions

1)Initial Setup
-Launch the app and grant any required permissions.
-Add and save at least one emergency contact.
-Customize your SOS message if desired.

2)Activating Emergency Mode
-Triple-Tap Power Button
-Ensure the app’s background monitoring service is running (it will  start automatically once you’ve added your first contact).
-Quickly press the device’s power button three times within three seconds.
-The app detects the triple tap, activates Emergency Mode, and immediately sends your SOS alert—no on-screen interaction needed.

3)Confirmation & Feedback
-After activation, you’ll see a brief notification confirming that your SOS message and location have been sent.
-If desired, shake your phone to re-verify activation.
